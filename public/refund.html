Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Trial

Your first 30 days are a free trial. You can cancel before the trial ends and no subscription charge is due.

Subscription payments

If a payment was duplicated, incorrectly charged, or a technical failure prevented access, contact support within seven days with the Paystack reference. We review legitimate requests and process approved refunds through the original payment channel when Paystack permits it.

Cancellation

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Access normally remains available until the paid period ends. A partial-period refund is not automatic, but exceptional cases can be reviewed by support.

Referral rewards

You earn ₦100 when someone signs up through your referral link, plus 10% of their first successful subscription payment. Your earnings appear in your Profile under Earnings & Wallet. Withdrawals can be requested once your available balance reaches ₦1,000; they are reviewed by an administrator and targeted for payment within three working days. Rewards are voided for self-referrals, duplicate accounts, chargebacks, refunded payments, or abuse. Pending wallet withdrawals may be paused while a payment is reviewed.

Contact
