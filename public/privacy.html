Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What we collect

For a premium account we store your email, display name, subscription status, trial dates, referral code, wallet ledger, withdrawal details, and payment references needed to provide the service. Reading notes, bookmarks, and prayers remain local unless you explicitly export or sync them.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store card numbers, PINs, or bank login details. Paystack sends us transaction status and reference data so we can activate or refund a subscription.

Activity and admin records

We keep limited security and business audit records for sign-ins, payment events, referrals, withdrawals, and administrator access. We do not secretly record every page you visit, your microphone audio, your location, or your private reading content. We may use aggregate, non-identifying product analytics only where enabled and disclosed.

AI study assistant

Questions sent to the optional AI study assistant are processed by the configured AI provider. Do not submit passwords, payment data, pastoral secrets, or sensitive personal information. The assistant is a study aid, not divine authority or professional advice.

Your choices

You may request account correction or deletion through WhatsApp support. Financial records may need to be retained for accounting and fraud prevention. Contact us at WhatsApp support.
