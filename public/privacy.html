Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

What we collect

For a premium account we store your email, display name, profile photo, subscription status, trial dates, referral code, wallet ledger, withdrawal details, and payment references needed to provide the service. Reading notes, bookmarks, and prayers remain local unless you explicitly export or sync them.

Signing in

You can sign in with an email and password, or with Google. With Google we receive only your email address, display name and profile picture, and only after you choose the Google button; we never read your contacts or Gmail. Google sign-in is verified on our server, and the administrator account is password-only so there is always exactly one way into it. If you tick Keep me signed in, your session lasts 30 days; otherwise it ends when you close the browser. We never store your password in the browser — only your email address, to fill the sign-in form.

Password recovery

Forgotten passwords are recovered with a one-time 6-digit code. The request raises an administrator alert; the code is valid once, expires after 30 minutes, and is stored only as a hash. Completing a reset signs out your other devices.

Referrals and payments you receive

Your referral code and the link built from it are yours to share. People who sign up through your link, and their first subscription payment, credit your wallet. We record who you referred, the amounts earned, and any withdrawal you request, including your bank details, so we can pay you and meet anti-fraud and accounting duties. Referral rewards are voided for self-referrals, duplicate accounts, chargebacks, refunded payments, or abuse.

Payments

Payments are processed by Paystack. We do not store card numbers, PINs, or bank login details. Paystack sends us transaction status and reference data so we can activate or refund a subscription.

Activity and admin records

We keep limited security and business audit records for sign-ins, payment events, referrals, withdrawals, and administrator access. We do not secretly record every page you visit, your microphone audio, your location, or your private reading content. We may use aggregate, non-identifying product analytics only where enabled and disclosed.

AI study assistant

Questions sent to the optional AI study assistant are processed by the configured AI provider. Do not submit passwords, payment data, pastoral secrets, or sensitive personal information. The assistant is a study aid, not divine authority or professional advice.

Your choices

You may request account correction or deletion through WhatsApp support. Financial records may need to be retained for accounting and fraud prevention. Contact us at WhatsApp support (or call/text {{WHATSAPP}}).
