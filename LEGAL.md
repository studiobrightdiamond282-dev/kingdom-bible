# KINGDOM BIBLE — Legal and Content Notes

## AI disclaimer

KINGDOM AI is a study aid. It does not speak for God, does not claim divine authority, and must not be treated as the voice of God, Jesus Christ, or the Holy Spirit. Interpretive notes may reflect common Christian readings; disputed questions should be identified and examined with Scripture and trusted teachers.

## Content disclaimer

Bible quotations are displayed without silent rewriting from the selected edition. Summaries, devotionals, prayers, definitions, and study explanations are clearly separate from Bible text.

## Privacy

The included local-first build stores personal profile settings, prayers, bookmarks, notes, highlights, reading activity, and study history in the user's browser. It sends presentation payloads only to the included presentation endpoint for live display synchronization. Production deployments require a complete jurisdiction-specific Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Policy, retention schedule, and deletion process.

## Copyright

KJV, ASV, and WEB are included as public-domain editions subject to applicable jurisdiction. Cross-reference data is used under CC BY 4.0 and should be attributed to CrossReferences.org / Treasury of Scripture Knowledge. Commercial copyrighted Bible translations and audio must not be added without written permission or a valid provider license.

## Community and child safety

Community features are not enabled in this build. A future release must include reporting, blocking, moderation, age-appropriate restrictions, privacy defaults, and legally required child-safety controls before public launch.
